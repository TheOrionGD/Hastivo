# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Participation in **Hastivo** is open to all. The project thrives on constructive
dialogue, mutual respect, shared dedication to accessibility, and deep commitment
to the Deaf, Hard-of-Hearing, and non-verbal communities across India and globally.
All interactions across issues, pull requests, discussions, community reviews, and
sign verification portals are expected to uphold the highest professional standards
and remain free from discrimination, harassment, or hostile behavior.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our
community include:

* **Accessibility First**: Prioritizing Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing inclusive language, respecting regional Indian Sign Language (ISL) dialects, and valuing input from native signers.
* **Demonstrating Empathy and Kindness**: Treating fellow contributors, researchers, signers, and users with dignity and care.
* **Respecting Divergent Viewpoints**: Respecting differing cultural, linguistic, and sign-syntax perspectives across northern, southern, eastern, and western ISL variations.
* **Constructive Collaboration**: Offering and gracefully accepting technical feedback, peer reviews on sign glosses, and UI/UX design critiques.
* **Accountability & Integrity**: Acknowledging errors promptly, adhering to zero-mock and real-data governance policies, and continually learning.
* **Focusing on Collective Impact**: Working for the betterment of accessible communication infrastructure for the 18+ million Deaf individuals in India.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks, or disparaging remarks targeting hearing loss, disabilities, gender identity, sexual orientation, ethnicity, caste, or religion.
* Public or private harassment or intimidation.
* Publishing others' private information (such as real names, personal contact numbers, physical addresses, or unanonymized sign recording clips) without explicit written consent.
* Dismissive or hostile treatment toward native sign language users or assistive technology needs.
* Conduct that damages the safety, trustworthiness, or inclusive mission of the Hastivo platform.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders and maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of
acceptable behavior. They possess full authority to take fair, impartial, and prompt corrective
action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (GitHub repositories, issue trackers,
discussions, community review boards, chat channels, and pull requests) as well as any public or
virtual forum where an individual represents the Hastivo project or its community.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining
the consequences for any action in violation of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Correction**: A private, written warning providing clarity on the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning**: A formal warning with explicit consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary Ban**: A temporary suspension from any interaction, commit privilege, or communication within the repository and Hastivo community spaces.
4. **Permanent Ban**: A permanent ban from all public repositories, organization memberships, and interaction channels associated with Hastivo.
